Who We Are

More Than a Project. A Movement for National Transformation.

WeNDI creates lasting multi-sectoral impact, expands social and economic opportunities, develops human capital and promotes Nigeria's values, strengths and global standing. We work across regions, sectors and generations to build the Nigeria we believe in.

01 / Pillar

People-Driven

Empowering communities, promoting inclusion and preserving cultural heritage as the foundation of national progress.

02 / Pillar

Country-Focused

Aligning every programme with Nigeria's socio-economic development priorities and our national vision.

03 / Pillar

Future-Building

Connecting culture, technology, enterprise and sustainability to create jobs and lasting opportunity.

WeNDI Mandate

A unified platform for national renewal.

Working hand-in-hand with government, private sector and citizens to deliver real, measurable, lasting impact.

Building Nigeria's Future

Eight sectors. One nation. Unlimited possibility.

We work across interconnected sectors that drive Nigeria's present and shape her future — from tourism and culture to enterprise, industry and innovation.

Showcasing Nigeria's heritage, destinations and creative energy to the world.
02

Film, music, fashion, design — powering Nigeria's cultural economy.
03

Skills, scholarships and pathways for Nigeria's young majority.
04

Research, digital infrastructure and homegrown breakthroughs.
05
Agriculture and Food Security
From farm to table — strengthening Nigeria's food systems.
06
Enterprise and SMEs
Backing Nigerian entrepreneurs with finance, markets and mentorship.
07
Environmental Sustainability
Climate resilience, clean energy and a green Nigerian economy.
08
Industry, Trade and Investment
Building Nigerian industry and connecting it to global markets.
