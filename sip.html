Special Intervention Programs

Welcome2Nigeria Project is primarily focused on the non-oil sector of the economy, especially in areas that require critical intervention and support through partnership and collaboration.

W2NP’s intervention mechanism harnesses innovation and creativity to expand and deepen national assets, drive growth and sustainable development, create positive change and increase access to diverse socio-economic opportunities.

Programmes

Special Intervention Programmes.

Our initiatives cut across culture, enterprise, agriculture and national reorientation. Scroll horizontally to explore the directory.

ENEX

EnjoyNigeria Expo

EnjoyNigeria Expo is a creative entertainment platform for promoting Nigeria’s performing, creative and visual arts, food, fashion, products, services, talent and skills. The expo is held annually in Abuja in November.

WtNEx

WelcometoNigeria Expo

WelcometoNigeria Expo is a national conference, business forum and exhibition for tourism vendors and stakeholders in Nigeria. WtNEx is an annual event held in September. Collaboration, partnership and sponsorship are welcomed.

GNEX

GrowNigeria Conference and Expo

GrowNigeria Conference and Expo brings together major stakeholders in the agriculture and agro-allied subsectors to discuss challenges hindering growth and propose solutions. The event is held in December and is open to collaboration and host partnerships.

ICRaMP

Int. Conference on Raw Materials and Products

The International Conference on Raw Materials and Products is held annually in October.

ICCI

Int. Conference & Expo on Cultural Industry

The International Conference and Expo on Cultural Industry is held in Abuja in January. The conference focuses on promoting Nigeria’s cultural industries.

ESB

Expo on Small Businesses

Expo on Small Businesses is held in Abuja in February. The programme promotes MSMEs, entrepreneurs, products and market opportunities.

EDMEP

EDMEP

EDMEP is a cultural empowerment programme focused on poverty alleviation in Nigeria’s rural communities. Its implementation is structured across the country’s six geopolitical regions.

NPCRS

National Programme for Cultural Re-adaptation for Schools

The National Programme for Cultural Re-adaptation for Schools is a cultural reorientation and promotion programme for schools across Nigeria. It operates throughout the year.

NPLH

No Place Like Home

No Place Like Home is a strategic orientation programme focused on changing perceptions about Nigeria. It is a pro-country programme that promotes pride in the motherland.
