About W2NP

Welcome2Nigeria Project (W2NP) is a Special Purpose Vehicle of Welcome to Nigeria Limited, designed as a strategic, people-centred socio-economic intervention and development initiative.

As a national development project, W2NP plays a pivotal role in driving economic growth, enhancing infrastructure and improving the overall well-being of citizens. Through collaborative partnerships with government stakeholders, the project addresses critical sectoral and societal challenges while fostering inclusive and sustainable progress.

W2NP is strategically positioned to stimulate economic development, support public initiatives and promote equitable growth. Its activities are aligned with national policies and global development objectives to create long-term benefits for Nigeria and its people.

Through innovative solutions, sustainable practices and efficient resource management, W2NP is committed to advancing national progress, generating employment opportunities and improving citizens’ quality of life.

Our Objectives

Five commitments. One Nigeria.

The objectives that guide every programme, partnership and intervention W2NP delivers.

01

Targeted Intervention Programmes

Implement targeted intervention programmes that foster opportunities for growth, enhance citizen engagement and drive sustainable development.

02

Strategic Partnerships and Research

Leverage strategic partnerships to conduct research and propose innovative solutions that strengthen key economic sectors and support sustained growth and stability.

03

Resource-Based Industries and FDI

Enhance business opportunities for resource-based industries to boost exports and attract Foreign Direct Investment, driving economic expansion.

04

Cultural Values and Heritage

Preserve and promote Nigeria’s cultural values and heritage, supporting traditional structures and aligning them with national development objectives.

05

National Service and Unity

Develop strategic programmes that inspire a renewed sense of national service and collective participation, fostering unity, growth and sustainable national development.

For Country, For People

The Welcome2Nigeria Project slogan, “For Country, For People,” encapsulates the core philosophy and objectives of the initiative. It reflects a steadfast commitment to delivering strategic services that foster growth and development while enhancing the quality of life for Nigerians.
